# Ecosystem, incentives and sustainability

Apply the [common envelope](README.md). Compare direct competitors, adjacent open-source projects, native functions, manual work and doing nothing. Inspect actual behavior, licenses, activity and maintenance evidence with retrieval dates/versions; record source-family overlap and access limits.

Return competitor/substitute and player/incentive tables, technical/economic/legal constraints, platform dependence, maintainer costs, switching costs, credible sequential responses and scenarios with observable triggers. Evaluate contribution to an existing project as an alternative. Explain useful value that may remain if a platform bundles the core feature. Use qualitative payoffs; no equilibrium claims without an explicit justified game. Do not turn popularity into proof of demand or volunteer labor into zero maintenance cost.
